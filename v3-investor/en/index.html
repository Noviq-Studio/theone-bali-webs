Frequently asked questions

What investors ask

What is Wyndham's relationship with the project?

Wyndham is the brand licensor, not the owner or the developer. The developer, PT. Magical Bali Villas, is an independent entity and is solely responsible for the ownership, development and operation. Use of the Registry Collection Hotels brand is covered by a limited licence that may be terminated in accordance with its terms.

What does a 25 + 15 year leasehold mean?

In Indonesia foreigners cannot hold freehold title to land: you acquire a right of use for a fixed term. Here it is 25 initial years with a further 15-year extension provided for. The exact extension terms are set out in the contract.

Is the 22% return guaranteed?

No. It is the maximum return published by the developer in its sales material and depends on occupancy, average rate, management costs and how Bali's tourism market evolves. The simulation on this page is indicative and does not constitute financial advice.

How does the 18-month payment plan work?

The project is sold with instalments spread over 18 months. The specific milestone schedule is set out in the official brochure and formalised in the purchase agreement.

Who manages the property and the rental?

Operations run to Wyndham standards, with access to the SynXis reservation system, the Wyndham Rewards programme and professional revenue management. Owners track their unit's activity from The One App.

When is it delivered and where exactly is it?

Estimated delivery in the third quarter of 2027, in Nusa Dua (Bukit peninsula, Bali): 30 minutes from the airport and 5–15 minutes from the area's leading beach clubs and luxury hotels.
